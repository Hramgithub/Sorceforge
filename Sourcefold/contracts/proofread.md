# Proofread Contract

Purpose: accept a Russian master that is reader-ready and semantically faithful.

## Inputs

- Faithful RU Draft.
- Accepted Source Text or source-linked units.
- Translation findings and terminology provenance.
- Proofreading configuration, model/tool versions, and editorial notes.

## Outputs

- Proofread RU Master.
- Semantic findings ledger.
- Russian-language findings ledger.
- RU master acceptance record.

## Required Preservation

- Meaning, mechanics, and data must not drift during Russian-language polish.
- Terminology changes must record rationale and applicability.
- Any unresolved ambiguity remains visible until accepted or waived.

## Acceptance Gate

Proofreading accepts the RU master only when no unresolved semantic or language
findings remain.

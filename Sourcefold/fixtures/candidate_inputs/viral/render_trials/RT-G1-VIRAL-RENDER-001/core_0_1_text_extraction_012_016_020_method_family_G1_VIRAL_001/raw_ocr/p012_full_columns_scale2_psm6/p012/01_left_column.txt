Em is 5’2” with a bob of strawberry blonde hair and an easy

smile. She wears Spektral Krew merchandise all the time and

Another Ghost Hunter Option. Em is from a rival ghost
hunting channel called The Phantom Posse. She is their
Spiritualist Medium who, unlike Corrina, genuinely believes
she has powers (she is very, very lightly psychic when compared
to someone like Enoch) as she does get general impressions
from places. The Posse were tipped off by Maya around a week
earlier about the island and they paid her handsomely for the
tip. They subsequently went to the island to film a 48-hour stay
there, but during their investigation were sickened by the Taint
of Hastalyk and all died... except Em who became completely
corrupted by the Taint and is now a hapless devotee of the
Grand Corruption — something she is completely unaware of.
She took the bodies of her former compatriots down to the
charnel pit and threw them in, feeding Hastalyk’s form, making
it draw nearer to being able to manifest.

She has a stand-offish relationship with Corrina that is
quite the opposite of the super fan version of Em. Corrina has
met her on a number of occasions and finds herself fascinated
and attracted to her in a way she can't really understand. Em
forgets her name and calls her Carla from time-to-time.

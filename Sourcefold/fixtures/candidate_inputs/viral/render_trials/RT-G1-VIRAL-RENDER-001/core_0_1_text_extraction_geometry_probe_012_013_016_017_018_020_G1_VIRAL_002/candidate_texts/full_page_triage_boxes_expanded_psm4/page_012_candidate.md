## creating_new_investigators_section

While this scenario is designed to be run with the pre-generated
Investigators provided, there is no reason that the players
cannot make their own. There are only a few important points
to keep in mind when doing so. The first is that the Investigators
should be making some sort of online ghost hunting show with
the goal of getting more views, subscriptions, patrons, and so
on. In other words, they should want to ‘go viral’.

The second important Investigator feature is that the host
should be strongly motivated to finish the stream at all costs.
While Marco’s motivation is money and fame, there’s no reason
why another host might be motivated by a rivalry with another
YouTube channel, an obsession with learning the truth about
the supernatural, or even driven to debunk the supernatural as
utter nonsense.

The third isn’t as critical, but it can be very helpful for at
least one of them to have The Sight (see p.60). This allows
them to learn more about the true nature of what’s going on,
while adding to the overall mood of the story. The Investigator
with The Sight could be the psychic from the group or not.
It could even be interesting to have someone who has been
faking psychic powers to suddenly experience real visions.

Other Investigator Options. Other options are possible,
though it would require some reworking by the Keeper. The
amount of reworking depends on how far the Investigators
stray from the scenario’s core premise. The important features
to include are plausible motivations for exploring the island,
and electronic devices that can be used by Hastalyk to control
the Investigators. The Keeper can use the ideas below, or come
up with their own.

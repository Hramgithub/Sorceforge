If the investigators do an internet search about Perla, a
Luck roll allows them to find a video made by an American
tourist who tried to stop one of the villagers from reaching
their home on Plague Night. The altercation left the tourist
in the hospital, and according to official records, the matter
was settled.

The video itself is disturbing. The villager in question was a
tough looking fisherman who was walking down a side street
when the American (a young man in his 20s) blocked the way
and started shoving the video camera in the villager’s face. The
fisherman tried to go around, but the young man kept laughing
and asking the fisherman where he was going in such a hurry,
repeating that he just wanted to ask some questions.

Things escalated quickly and suddenly the camera was still
running on the ground as it recorded the fisherman repeatedly
punching the tourist. It ends after the fisherman storms off and
the tourist crawls over to the camera to shut it off. According to
the video, this incident happened in 2002.

Keeper’s Notes

If the investigators try to find the fisherman, they'll have no
luck. He died from cancer about three years ago. None of the
villagers recall the incident, nor is there any record of it.

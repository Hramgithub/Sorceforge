While the investigators are in Perla, there are a number of
things that they can learn by talking to the locals or noticing
details around the village. The Keeper can roll randomly to
determine which bits of information they acquire, or they
can choose whichever makes the most sense. Of course the
Keeper can always improvise new ones to suit their version
of the scenario, particularly if they are using alternative
investigators rather than the Spektral Krew.

Note that most of these aren't true, or are mingled with
half-truths. When hearing a rumor, the Keeper should have
the investigators make an Intelligence check to see it for the

nonsense it is.

Rumors

Roll Rumor
1 The real reason the island was raided back in the 1950s
was because there was some sort of Satanic cult that

was out there sacrificing children. [Rational Explanation:
There have been very few actual Satanic cults discovered,
and none that actually killed children. This sounds like the
same old Satanic panic from the 1980s.]

2. Fishermen report seeing ghost lights on the island at
night this time of year. They say it’s the spirits of dead
children looking for their parents to take them home.
[Rational Explanation: These are the sorts of stories told
by sailors since humans first took to the sea. The lights
could easily be explained as teenagers drinking on the
island or lights from other boats seen through the trees.]

3. The hospital was just a front, and that the government
was engineering biological weapons. It was shut down
when a virus got out and killed everyone on the island.
[Rational Explanation: While not impossible, it’s highly
unlikely that Italy or the United States would have
violated the Geneva Convention so blatantly. It’s even
more unlikely that it wouldn’t have come to light by now.]

4 The island is populated by giant mutant rats. [Rational
Explanation: While not impossible, it’s not a large island,
which would make it unlikely for a population of any large
mammals (mutant or otherwise) to thrive there.]

5 Parents will sometimes take a boat out to the island if
their child is particularly ill to leave on offering on the
Weeping Bridge. [Rational Explanation: This sounds plausible
enough, and the investigators are likely to have this ramor
confirmed by many of the villagers. This one is true.]

6 Teenagers will occasionally take a boat for a joy ride
out to the island to party at a place called Beggar’s
Cove. It’s supposedly located around the far side on the
small island. [Rational Explanation: This sounds plausible,
and could explain some of the other rumors. If the
investigators speak to any of the teens loitering at the docks,
they’ll happily confirm this. This one is obviously true.]

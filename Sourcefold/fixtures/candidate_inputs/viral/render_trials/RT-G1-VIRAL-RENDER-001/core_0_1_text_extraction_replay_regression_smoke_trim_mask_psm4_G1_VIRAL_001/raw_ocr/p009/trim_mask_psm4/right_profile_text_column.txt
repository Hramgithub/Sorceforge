Peter Wharton (Investigator)

Peter is a career criminal and former heroin addict who fell
into his current job as cameraman for Spektral Krew when
he went to sell a stolen laptop and digital camera to Marco.
He had bought and sold so many of these over the years
that when asked by Marco to demonstrate how it worked,
he easily understood what to do, and Marco offered him a
job on the spot which he accepted as nobody had ever done
that for him before. In order to keep the cash flowing, for
Spektral Krew and thus himself, he secretly sets up fake
evidence and triggers it at opportune moments during the
investigations — using things like fishing line to move things
off camera, palming small objects and leaving them in other
places, then spotting them with shock on his face, leaving
superchat messages on the stream pointing out his ‘activity’
using a false account, and the like. Peter’s player should be
encouraged to be inventive with his fake evidence, as well as
making sure he doesn’t get caught.

Peter is a man of few words and although appreciative of
his job, he is always looking for the next thing to steal as it is
a habit for him (he is a borderline kleptomaniac). Not easily
phased. Likes Enoch as he sees him as a bit of a kindred spirit.

APPEARANCE

Peter (39) is 5°9” and rakishly thin with slightly unkempt hair.

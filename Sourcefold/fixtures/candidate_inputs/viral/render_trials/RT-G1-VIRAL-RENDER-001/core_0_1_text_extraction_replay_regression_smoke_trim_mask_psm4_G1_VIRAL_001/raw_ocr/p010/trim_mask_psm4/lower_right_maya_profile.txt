Maya Adeduwon (NPC)

She was in a car accident when she was younger which left her
wheelchair-bound. She is as sharp as a tack, incredibly smart,
and quite sassy when she needs to be. She has a habit of
calling everyone ‘babez’ (for example, “Marco babez, what do
you want me to say?”). She found out about Isola di Malamente
from an online contact.

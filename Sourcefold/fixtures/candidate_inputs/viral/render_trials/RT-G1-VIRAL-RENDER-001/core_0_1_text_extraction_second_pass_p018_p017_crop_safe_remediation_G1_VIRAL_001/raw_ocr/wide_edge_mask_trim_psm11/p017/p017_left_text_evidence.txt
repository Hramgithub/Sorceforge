One Night in Perla is a new prologue that expands on the

original scenario by giving the investigators an opportunity to

explore the nearby fishing village of Perla. This offers them

an opportunity to learn more about the history and lore of

Isola di Malamente, and bear witness to the ancient festival of

Lepurazione di Morbus (The Purging of Morbus).

The Purging of Morbus. The ceremony lasts for three days

and begins on the first night with I/ Rogo della Malattia (The

Burning of the Disease). This involves burning an effigy of

the demon Morbus which is supposed to banish him from the

village. They believe that this causes Morbus to retaliate, so on

the second night, known as Notte della Peste (Plague Night),

they lock themselves in their homes and place talismans at all

of the possible means of entry to trap any illness that might try

to get inside. The third and final night is La Purificazione (The

Purification), and involves burning all of the talismans in the

piazza, followed by a celebration that goes well into the night.

The people of Perla take the festival very seriously, so any

attempt to interfere will be met first with strong resistance,

and if necessary, violence. Of course if a bunch of foolish

Americans want to get themselves killed, that’s their business,

but if anyone tries to interfere with the festival, especially on

Plague Night, it won’t go well for them.

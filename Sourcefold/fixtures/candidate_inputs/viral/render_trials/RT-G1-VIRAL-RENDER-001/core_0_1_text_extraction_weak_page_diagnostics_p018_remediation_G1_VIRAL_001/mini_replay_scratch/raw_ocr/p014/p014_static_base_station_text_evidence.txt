Static

These bits of equipment are set up early on and, with the

exception of the drone, remain at a single location.

Base Station

This is a custom industrial laptop with a Wi-Fi hub and satellite
uplink set up for live streaming. A deep cycle battery unit supplies
enough power for up to 12 hours of continuous broadcasting.

Keeper’s Notes
At the Keeper’s discretion, other devices may be charged off
the base station’s battery, but this obviously takes time that

the Investigators may not have.

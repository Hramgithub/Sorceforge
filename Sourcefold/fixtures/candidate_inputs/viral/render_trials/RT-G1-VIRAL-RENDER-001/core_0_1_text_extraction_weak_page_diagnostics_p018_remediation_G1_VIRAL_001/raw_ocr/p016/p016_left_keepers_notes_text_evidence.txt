Keeper’s Notes

The public is unaware of the cult of Hastalyk, or the intense
firefight that took place when Italian military forces stormed
the island in 1954. When the Italian government first received
information regarding the abuse of patients, it immediately sent
members of the social services to investigate with a police escort.
At first, the hospital administrator seemed cooperative, but when
the investigators discovered fresh remains in the cremation pit,
they ordered the hospital shut down. Subsequently, the social
services investigators and their police escort were lured into the
chapel where they were slaughtered by the cultists, though one
of the police officers did manage to contact their seaplane which
was waiting at the dock. The quick-thinking pilot took off and
managed to radio the authorities.

Meanwhile, the bodies of the investigators were quickly
burned, and realizing that the end was nigh, the cult murdered
the non-cult members of the hospital staff and euthanized all
of the patients. Their remains were brought into the secret
catacombs beneath the chapel.

While this was happening, the authorities reviewed the
pilot’s unsettling report and decided that they would have to
use the military to take the island. Considering the nature of the
situation, they called in the DSS (Divisione Sicurezza Speciale),
a secret division within the Italian government that handles
sensitive issues of national security. In a matter of hours, a
squadron of helicopters was flying to Isola di Malamente with a
heavily armed strike team.

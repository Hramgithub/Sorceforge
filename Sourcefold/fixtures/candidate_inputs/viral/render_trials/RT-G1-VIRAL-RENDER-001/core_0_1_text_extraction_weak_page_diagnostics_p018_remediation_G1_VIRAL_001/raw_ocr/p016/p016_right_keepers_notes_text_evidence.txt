The two last Plague Monks had fled into the secret shrine to
perform a dark ritual that involved one of the hospital’s infants
reserved for that purpose. Using the luramentum Corruptionis,
one prepared the other to be the final sacrifice. They waited
until the interlopers were gone and made their way to the
Charnel Pit where the final sacrifice was hung by the neck
above the pit until the time was right. The other went back to
the hidden shrine with the book where he killed himself with a
bullet through the temple, thus completing the ritual.

The last Plague Monk has since watched over the island
as a shadowy presence, feeding more tainted flesh into the
Charnel Pit whenever possible in preparation for the great day
when Hastalyk would be born.

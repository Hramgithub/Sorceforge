Communication. Cell phone service is decent in Perla
offering 3G speeds, though there doesn’t appear to be any
public Wifi options available anywhere. There are however <
few public phone booths that are surprisingly still in service
though of course the Krew can simply use their own cel
phones or the phone at the hotel if they wish.

Local radio stations mainly play classical music or Italiar
favorites from the 40s and 50s. Most of the locals watch one
of the three broadcast television channels, one of which has
American reruns (mostly from the 70s, 80s, and 90s) every
night at 8pm. While the hotel doesn’t have cable or Wifi, the
3G signal is good enough that the investigators can access

the internet and their own streaming media if they wish.
